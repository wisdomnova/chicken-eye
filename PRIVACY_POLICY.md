# Privacy Policy for Chicken Eye

**Last updated:** September 13, 2026

## Overview

Chicken Eye ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Extension handles user information.

## Data Collection and Usage

- **No Personal Data Collected**: Chicken Eye does not collect, store, track, transmit, or sell any personal data, sensitive user data, browsing history, form inputs, or passwords.
- **Local Operation**: All functionality of the Extension runs entirely within your browser on your local device. No network requests are made to external servers or third-party analytics services.
- **Passwords**: When you click the visibility toggle, the Extension switches the HTML input field type between `password` and `text` locally in your DOM. The Extension never reads, captures, stores, or logs the contents of any input fields or passwords.
- **Settings Storage**: The Extension uses Chrome's `storage.sync` API solely to persist your local preference (enabled/disabled state). This preference is stored in your personal Google Chrome profile.

## Third-Party Services

Chicken Eye does not integrate with any third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or telemetry services.
